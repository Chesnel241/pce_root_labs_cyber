Scenario:
We have deployed an Ingress controller for our internal Kubernetes cluster.
It exposes a public application at `http://localhost:8080/app/`.
However, we suspect there is a routing misconfiguration that might allow access to the internal administration panel, which is located at the `/admin` path on the backend service.

Your objective is to exploit the Ingress routing misconfiguration to retrieve the flag from the internal `/admin` endpoint.
