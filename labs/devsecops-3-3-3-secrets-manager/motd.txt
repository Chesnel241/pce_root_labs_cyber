PCE Root Labs - DevSecOps 3.3.3: Migrer vers Secrets Manager
========================================================================

Welcome, Analyst!

Your company has decided to improve security by removing all hardcoded
secrets from the source code. Your task is to update 'app.py'.

1. Remove the hardcoded DB_PASS ("SuperSecretPassword123!") from app.py.
2. Use the 'boto3' library to fetch the secret instead.
3. You must use the secretsmanager client:
   client = boto3.client('secretsmanager')
4. And call get_secret_value with the correct SecretId:
   client.get_secret_value(SecretId='prod/db/password')

When you are finished, run 'verify.sh' to check your work and get your flag.
Good luck!
