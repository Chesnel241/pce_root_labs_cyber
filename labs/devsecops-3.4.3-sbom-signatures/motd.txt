Welcome to the Image Security Lab!

As part of our DevSecOps pipeline, we need to ensure that all container images 
are properly signed and that we maintain an accurate Software Bill of Materials (SBOM).

We have an image artifact. Your tasks:
1. Review the generated SBOM (sbom.json) to find the first part of the flag hidden in a vulnerable component's version.
2. Use the 'cosign' tool to verify the image 'registry.local/myapp:v1.0' using 'cosign.pub'.
   (Command: cosign verify --key cosign.pub registry.local/myapp:v1.0)
   
Concatenate the two parts to get the full flag!
Format: PCE{part1_part2}
