Welcome to the Jenkins Pipeline Audit Lab!
====================================================================

Scenario:
A junior developer has created a CI/CD pipeline using Jenkins to build
and deploy our application. However, the security team flagged it for 
multiple bad practices and vulnerabilities.

Your task:
1. Audit the 'Jenkinsfile' in your home directory.
2. Identify and fix security issues (e.g., hardcoded credentials, 
   insecure curls, running as root).
3. Run './check_pipeline.py' to verify your fixes. If the pipeline 
   is secure, you will receive the flag!

Good luck!
