Welcome to the IAM Dormant Access Keys Lab!

Scenario:
You are an auditor reviewing the AWS IAM access keys for your organization.
Security policy dictates that any Access Key older than 90 days which has not been used in the last 90 days must be identified and removed.

Your task:
Analyze the provided 'access_keys_audit.csv' in your home directory.
Find the Access Key ID of the active access key that violates this policy (created > 90 days ago AND last used > 90 days ago OR never used). Note: ignore inactive keys.

The flag format is PCE{ACCESS_KEY_ID}.
Example: PCE{AKIA1234567890ABCDEF}

Good luck!
