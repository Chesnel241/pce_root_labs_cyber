Scenario:
Our primary AWS CloudTrail logging was abruptly disabled. 
Fortunately, the API call that disabled it was logged just in time.

Task:
Analyze the 'cloudtrail_logs.json' file in your home directory.
Identify the event where CloudTrail was stopped.
The flag is embedded within the 'requestParameters.name' of that event.

Good luck!
