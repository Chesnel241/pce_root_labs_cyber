Welcome to the Kubernetes Admission Controller / PSP Lab!

You are tasked with assessing the security of a Kubernetes cluster. 
The cluster administrators have implemented a Pod Security Policy (PSP) 
to prevent malicious pods from taking over the nodes. 

Your objective:
Bypass the Admission Controller's restrictions and read the secret flag 
from the underlying node's filesystem.

Tools available:
- kubectl (mocked for this lab to simulate pod creation)
- vi/nano for creating YAML manifests

Hint: Check what the PSP actually blocks, and find what it forgot to block!
