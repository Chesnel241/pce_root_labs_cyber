========================================================================
Welcome to the Cross-Account Role Trust Abuse Lab
========================================================================

Scenario:
You have compromised AWS credentials for the account '111122223333' 
(the attacker account).

During recon, you found a trust policy for a role in a partner account 
'999988887777' named 'VulnerableRole'. The trust policy incorrectly 
trusts the entire '111122223333' account instead of a specific role, 
meaning any user in '111122223333' can assume it.

Goal:
1. Assume the 'VulnerableRole' in account '999988887777'.
   (Role ARN: arn:aws:iam::999988887777:role/VulnerableRole)
2. Use the assumed credentials to list S3 buckets.
3. Find and download the flag.txt.

Useful commands:
- aws sts assume-role --role-arn <ARN> --role-session-name <NAME>
- export AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID=...
- export AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY=...
- export AWS_SESSION_TOKEN=...
- aws s3 ls
- aws s3 cp s3://<bucket>/flag.txt .
