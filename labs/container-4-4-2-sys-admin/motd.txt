Welcome to the SYS_ADMIN Capability Lab!

You are logged into a simulated container. The container was poorly configured
and started with excessive privileges.

Your objective:
1. Verify the capabilities available to you (e.g. using `capsh --print`).
2. Identify available block devices attached from the host (e.g. using `lsblk`).
3. Leverage your privileges to mount the host's root filesystem and find the flag.

Note: This is a simulated offline environment. Standard Linux tools like `mount`,
`lsblk`, and `capsh` have been mocked for this exercise.
