===============================================================
               IMDSv2 Header Injection Lab
===============================================================

Scenario:
You have discovered a vulnerable internal web application that 
acts as an SSRF proxy. It is running locally on port 8080.

The cloud environment has been upgraded to IMDSv2, meaning you 
must supply specific HTTP headers to access the metadata service.
For this lab, the IMDS service is mock-hosted at:
http://127.0.0.1:9090/

Your Goal:
1. Exploit the SSRF endpoint at http://127.0.0.1:8080/fetch.
2. Leverage header injection to bypass IMDSv2 restrictions.
3. Retrieve the IAM credentials at:
   /latest/meta-data/iam/security-credentials/admin

The flag is embedded in the returned AccessKeyId.

Good luck!
