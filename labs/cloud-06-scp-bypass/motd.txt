Welcome to the SCP Lab!

You are an IAM administrator for your organization. However, the root account has applied a Service Control Policy (SCP) that restricts your actions.
You need to launch an EC2 instance to process some data, but your `aws ec2 run-instances` command keeps getting AccessDenied.

Use the mock `aws` CLI to investigate the organization's policies and find a way to launch your instance.

Available mock commands:
- aws organizations list-policies --filter SERVICE_CONTROL_POLICY
- aws organizations describe-policy --policy-id <id>
- aws ec2 run-instances [--region <region>]
